Deficits & Risks

A calorie deficit is how you lose fat — but bigger isn't better. Past a point you lose more muscle, feel worse, and rebound harder.

Sustainable · 10–20% below maintenance
Steady fat loss, energy left for training, muscle preserved. Right for most people most of the time.
Aggressive · 20–25%
Faster loss. Workable for a shorter push if protein is high and sleep is dialed in, but training and recovery start to suffer. Not for months on end.
Severe · 25%+ or under ~1,200 cal
Muscle loss, hormonal disruption, poor recovery, mood and sleep problems, and a strong rebound risk. Rarely worth it, and not something to run without a specific reason and ideally professional guidance.
Protein protects you
In any deficit, high protein (roughly 0.8–1g per lb of bodyweight) is what keeps the weight coming off fat instead of muscle.
Diet breaks are a tool
A week or two at maintenance during a long cut can restore energy and adherence without erasing your progress.

General education, not medical advice. If you have a history of disordered eating or any health condition, talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before running an aggressive cut.
